Facilitator & floor team — quick FAQ

Antarious AI · PKSF workshop hands-on block. Keep answers short and consistent.

Login & access

“I can’t log in.”
Check VPN / network policy first. Retry browser refresh. Try Chromium-based browser. If credentials fail, confirm role assignment — escalate to IT contact (pre-printed on table card).
“Wrong dashboard / I don’t see my agent.”
Role-based views are expected. Ask them to use the global Programme Intelligence or the agent list from the agenda. If still blocked, pair with a colleague who has access for Exercise 3 only.
“Session timed out.”
Re-login; unsaved draft may be lost — use Exercise 2 prompt again from the prompt library.

During exercises

“The answer looks wrong.”
That’s a teaching moment: verify against professional judgement. Ask: “What would you check in MIS or field records?” Never argue with the model — validate sources.
“Blank page / spinning.”
Wait 60s. Hard refresh. Switch browser. If outage is widespread, facilitator switches to offline Glass Box demo + worksheet (printed backup).
“Finished early.”
Give them the medium prompt for the same agent, or the “challenge prompt” for their role from prompt-library.json.

Escalation

When to stop helping one participant
After 3 minutes, pair them with table captain or move to shared screen demo row — don’t lose the room.
